Clay Tile Colors

Clay tile color comes from the natural clay and the kiln firing itself, not a surface coating, so it won’t peel and resists fading for decades. Options range from classic terracotta to warm earth-tone blends and glazed finishes.

Natural Vs. Glazed Clay Tile

Natural (unglazed) tile has a soft, matte, earthy look that weathers gracefully. Glazed tile adds a glass-like finish that comes in brighter colors, sheds dirt and moisture more easily, and holds its sheen for years. Lighter terracotta and buff tones also reflect more of Houston’s sun.
