What Is A Siding Inspection?

A siding inspection is a professional check of your home’s entire exterior cladding, including panels, trim, seams, attachment points and the sealant around windows and doors. The goal is to find damage, moisture and wear early, so you know whether your siding needs a simple repair, a partial replacement or nothing at all.

In Houston’s humid climate, small gaps can let moisture behind the siding, where it quietly causes rot and mold. An inspection catches those problems while they’re still small.
