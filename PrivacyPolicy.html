Privacy Policy

The Crescent Builders respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when filling out our forms, requesting services, or contacting us. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Service request details
  • Any other information you provide

We may also collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, and device information when you visit our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to service inquiries
  • Schedule roofing and exterior inspections or consultations
  • Provide estimates and project updates
  • Communicate regarding your service request
  • Improve our services and customer experience

3. SMS and Phone Communications

By submitting your information through our website or contact forms, you consent to receive communications from The Crescent Builders via phone calls, SMS text messages, and automated or AI-assisted calls related to your inquiry, service request, appointment scheduling, estimates, or follow-up communication.

If you opt in to receive marketing messages, you may also receive promotional offers, service updates, or special discounts from The Crescent Builders.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

4. Consent and Opt-In

Customers provide consent to receive communications by submitting their information through our website forms and agreeing to be contacted regarding their inquiry. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with service providers that assist us in operating our business, but only to the extent necessary to fulfill those services.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or analytics tools to improve website functionality and understand visitor behavior. These tools do not collect sensitive personal information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where applicable by law.
