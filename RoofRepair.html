Will Insurance Cover My Roof Repairs?

Most Texas homeowners insurance policies cover sudden damage from wind, hail and storms, but not normal wear and tear or an aging roof. What’s covered depends on your policy, your deductible (many Texas policies have a separate wind and hail deductible) and the condition of your roof before the storm. After a free inspection, we give you a detailed report with photos that you can share with your insurance adjuster.

How To Report Storm Damage To Your Insurance Company

Act quickly after a storm. Once it’s safe, take clear photos of the damage inside and outside your home, and cover any openings with a tarp to prevent further damage (keep your receipts). Contact your insurance company as soon as possible to open a claim, then schedule a free inspection with The Crescent Builders — we’ll document every issue so your claim has the evidence it needs.
