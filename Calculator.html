How Roof Material And Size Affect Your Cost

The material you choose and the size of your roof are the biggest factors in your total cost. Asphalt shingles are a budget-friendly, durable option and the most popular roofing material in the U.S. A premium material like metal costs more upfront in exchange for a longer lifespan and better heat reflection. And the larger your roof’s surface area, the more material and labor it takes.

Will Insurance Cover My Roof Replacement?

In many cases, insurance will cover a roof replacement if the damage is caused by events outside your control, like hail, high winds, hurricanes or falling trees, all common in Houston. Coverage varies widely by policy, including your wind and hail deductible, so review your coverage details. Damage from general wear and tear or an aging roof typically isn’t covered.
